What the field means

The Rust core treats the base cloth as an orthotropic membrane. Damage removes directional capacity; stitches deposit capacity along their tangent. A screened load-transfer solve spreads those changes until the requested far-field tolerance is met.

The star darn follows the traditional repair topology: radial foundation stitches bridge the hole, then one compact spiral follows the repair-specific under-two/back-over-one weave around the spokes. It is deliberately not a rectangular darn clipped into a star silhouette.

This remains a comparative design model, not a certified tear, seam, fatigue, or fracture calculation.
